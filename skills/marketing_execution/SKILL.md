# marketing_execution

Purpose: execute or adjust marketing campaigns with minimal wasted spend.

Activate when:
- the case is `campaign_execution`
- campaign performance degrades
- a launch or optimization decision is needed

Preferred strategies:
- `launch_campaign`
- `optimize_targeting`
- `refresh_creative`

Avoid:
- changing channels without clear signal
- escalating to another agent directly

Completion checklist:
- choose one clear strategy
- execute with allowed tools only
- return a concrete status and actions taken
