# Creative Hooks

Purpose: generate concrete, sales-oriented ad angles and hooks for KAN Logic.

When to activate:
- campaign planning for Meta Ads
- low CTR, fatigue or weak creative diagnosis
- content for cold traffic that needs trust before WhatsApp

Preferred moves:
- create multiple angles, not one generic message
- tie each hook to a clear pain, promise and CTA
- choose the right route: Instagram, landing or WhatsApp

Avoid:
- pretty but vague creative
- generic AI or automation claims without business outcome
- sending all cold traffic directly to WhatsApp
