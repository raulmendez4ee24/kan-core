# Offer Analysis

Purpose: understand what KAN Logic should sell, to whom, and why now.

When to activate:
- a prospect asks for pricing, demo, implementation or "more info"
- a marketing case needs a sharper offer before writing creative

Preferred moves:
- identify the buyer's operational bottleneck
- map the request to the best KAN Logic offer or plan
- surface objections early

Avoid:
- generic "we help businesses grow" language
- pushing WhatsApp too early on cold traffic
- treating every lead as enterprise
