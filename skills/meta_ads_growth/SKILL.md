# meta_ads_growth

Purpose: grow Meta Ads profitability by diagnosing creative, offer, funnel and follow-up bottlenecks before scaling spend.

Activate when:
- the case is `campaign_execution`
- Facebook or Instagram Ads are producing traffic but weak sales
- cost rises after scaling budget
- leads start conversations but do not reply or book

Core rules:
- treat Meta as a traffic and signal engine, not the whole sales system
- prioritize creative, offer and follow-up before heavy audience tweaks
- use CTR as an early interest signal in the first 24-48 hours
- do not scale spend hard until backend conversion is stable

Preferred strategies:
- `refresh_creative`
- `tighten_offer`
- `repair_whatsapp_followup`
- `stabilize_scaling`
- `qualify_leads_harder`
- `build_retargeting`
- `launch_campaign`

Metric guardrails:
- early CTR goal: `> 3%`
- weak CTR: `< 1%`
- if traffic arrives but reply rate is weak, treat follow-up as the bottleneck
- if spend grows but sales stay flat, freeze scaling and inspect lead quality plus close rate

Common bottlenecks:
- ad attracts curiosity instead of buying intent
- first WhatsApp reply is generic or slow
- offer is unclear or low urgency
- scaling happens before winning creatives are stable
- campaign optimizes for cheap conversations instead of revenue

Avoid:
- over-trusting manual targeting when creative is weak
- raising budget while reply rate, booking rate or close rate are deteriorating
- blaming the channel before checking response speed and script quality

Completion checklist:
- identify the primary funnel bottleneck
- choose one clear marketing strategy
- return concrete recommendations for ad, offer and follow-up
- execute only with allowed tools
